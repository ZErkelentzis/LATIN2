Nodes:
Rectangles with rounded edges: theories
Normal rectangles: Collections of theories.

Colours (for theories):
Orange: untyped
Green: typed
Pink: soft-typed
Purple: set theory
Blue: transformations

Arrows:
____> (normal arrows): include (or fixmeta import), points towards the theory that includes. In dependency graph a dependency.
----> (dashed arrows): realize, points towards the theory that realizes.
|____> (thick arrow): views, points towards the theory that is realized with the view.
|----> (thick dashed arrows): structure or total structure, points towards the theory formalizing the structure.
|_-_-> (thick black arrow): structure view is used.
