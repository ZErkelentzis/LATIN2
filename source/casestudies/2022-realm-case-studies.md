# Three Case Studies on Realms

This file provides an entry point for the formalizations that are part of the paper Three Case Studies on Realms.
The formulations are integrated with the heaily modular LATIN2 library and are split over the following files.

### Lattices

The algebraic formulation:
* https://gl.mathhub.info/MMT/LATIN2/-/tree/devel/source/domain_theories/algebra/magmas.mmt  
* https://gl.mathhub.info/MMT/LATIN2/-/tree/devel/source/domain_theories/algebra/lattices.mmt  

The order-based formulation:
* https://gl.mathhub.info/MMT/LATIN2/-/blob/devel/source/domain_theories/relations/orders.mmt  
* https://gl.mathhub.info/MMT/LATIN2/-/blob/devel/source/domain_theories/relations/lattices.mmt
