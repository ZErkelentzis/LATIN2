# Formalizations of "Modular Formalization of Formal Systems"

"Modular Formalization of Formal Systems" by Florian Rabe and Navid Roux is a paper submitted to [ITP 2021](http://easyconferences.eu/itp2021/).

This is a self-contained formalization closely following the submitted paper.

Many parts for this formalization have been extracted and adapted from the surrounding [LATIN2 archive](https://gl.mathhub.info/MMT/LATIN2/-/tree/devel) to be closer to the paper and easier to understand.
For example, LATIN2 employs much more fine-grained theory graphs split over many directories and files and uses MMT features not discussed in the paper (e.g. named structures).
